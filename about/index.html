Gulmohar

Gulmohar is an observational painter exploring botanical forms, coastal light, and spatial memory.

Statement

My work investigates the quiet architecture of trees and open spaces—how light filters through flowering canopies and foliage across changing times of day. By situating paintings within an immersive botanical garden, each canvas dialogues directly with nature, shadows, and silence.

Biography

Working primarily in oil, linen, and gouache, Gulmohar’s practice centers on direct observation and architectural balance. Based between studio and garden, the paintings reflect seasonal transitions, dappled light, and the enduring presence of native trees.
