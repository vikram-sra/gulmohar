Design notes

The original sketch traced a continuous ring path around the plot — a loop walked without ever choosing a direction. This revision removes it. Four paths now radiate from the sitting circle beneath the gulmohar, so the centrepiece tree doubles as the garden's junction: back to the entry, left to the pond, right to the maple, forward to the gazebo.
